# Asset and copyright policy

GTA Bangla Hub does not bundle Rockstar Games screenshots, trailer frames, logos, music, fonts, maps, or other copyrighted game assets. The visual system primarily uses original HTML, CSS gradients, typography, and project-owned SVG artwork.

The home page may display a small, spoiler-safe selection from Rockstar Games' official GTA VI Artwork & Wallpapers gallery. Those images load directly from `www.rockstargames.com`; they are not copied into this repository, offered for download, modified, or used as GTA Bangla Hub branding. Each item names the official title, credits Rockstar Games / Take-Two Interactive, links to the full official gallery, and carries the site's independent-fan-project disclaimer.

**Rights record — reviewed 28 November 2026:** owner: Rockstar Games / Take-Two Interactive; source: `https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/media/artwork-wallpapers`; selected use: three remote promotional images in a non-commercial, spoiler-free fan context; redistribution: disabled; removal contact: the published copyright/takedown workflow. Rockstar's fan-material policy generally permits occasional non-commercial fan use but reserves takedown rights and may require licensing for digital-publishing uses. This limited display must be removed or separately licensed if the site becomes commercial, an owner objects, or the policy changes.

## Publishing gate

Before adding any third-party asset, an editor must record its source, owner, licence or permission basis, allowed uses, required attribution, and review date. Linking or loading from an official source is preferred to copying its media. A descriptive summary must never become a substitute download or redistribution of the original work.

Assets are rejected when their rights are unclear, they originate from a leak or private source, they expose personal information, or their use would distribute a substantial part of a protected work. Fair-dealing or similar exceptions require a documented editorial/legal review rather than assumption.

## Accessibility and operations

Every informative image needs meaningful Bengali alternative text; decorative images use an empty `alt`. Optimized dimensions and formats must meet the performance budgets. Copyright or takedown requests go through the published Community Desk process, and disputed assets are unpublished while the claim is reviewed.
